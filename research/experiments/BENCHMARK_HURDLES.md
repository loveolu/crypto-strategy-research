## Accounting audit

The supplied display is useful as a competitive hurdle but not one internally
consistent backtest: 2023 is a fixed-rule/in-sample year, while 2024–26 and displayed
drawdowns come from the rolling walk-forward OOS stream, whose first test begins in
2024. Compounding those columns creates a hybrid record.

For honest comparisons, every challenger must be shown against all three views:

1. the exact user/Claude displayed hurdle above;
2. the existing rolling walk-forward B2 record (2024–2026 only: +40.6% total,
   13.9% annualized, Sharpe 1.23, −5.5% drawdown);
3. a consistent fixed-rule B2 run over 2023-01-22→2026-09-01: 2023 +15.04%,
   2024 +56.48%, 2025 +5.90%, 2026 +0.73%, +92.03% total, −10.42% drawdown.

A challenger does not win through leverage, a different universe, gross returns,
mixed validation streams, or a weaker drawdown/robustness profile. It must report the
same chronological periods, costs, concentration, delays, recent windows and sample
size. A superior return with materially worse drawdown is a trade-off, not a win.
