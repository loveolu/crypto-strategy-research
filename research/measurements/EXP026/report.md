# CRYPTO-EXP-026 — cross-venue return convergence

**Verdict: REJECTED on TRAIN before placebo; held-out remained sealed.**

The rule traded exceptional same-coin 4h return divergence between Kraken and OKX,
then entered the lagging direction on OKX at the next open for one bar. It produced
1,074 TRAIN trades with strong sample breadth (143–166 trades per coin).

Gross expectancy was only +6.25 bps/trade versus 14.23 bps measured expected
round-trip cost. Long signals earned +8.45 gross bps and short signals +4.12 gross
bps, so direction symmetry passed, but neither side had enough economic magnitude.
After costs the portfolio returned −12.13%, with −7.98 bps/trade, Sharpe −0.77,
PF .88 and maximum drawdown −17.17%. Only two of seven coins were net-positive.

The evidence is consistent with rapid cross-venue arbitrage: by the time both 4h
candles are complete and a realistic next-open order can execute, the residual
convergence is smaller than trading friction. Because economic-size and breadth
prerequisites failed, the circular-shift placebo and held-out period were not opened.
No shorter holding time, threshold, direction subset or timestamp tolerance will be
mined from these outcomes.
