# CRYPTO-EXP-019 — cross-venue B2 portfolio audit

**Verdict: REJECTED — no meaningful venue diversification.** This was a
retrospective audit because Kraken's full B2 outcome had already been viewed; it is
not new OOS evidence.

The frozen OKX and Kraken seven-alt B2 implementations had 0.966 correlation on 4h
exit returns and 0.970 correlation on daily returns. A fixed 50/50 allocation returned
+93.77% (+20.12% annualized), with Sharpe 1.48 and maximum drawdown −13.15%.
Calendar returns were +12.68% in 2023, +62.94% in 2024, +4.87% in 2025 and +0.64%
through 2026-09-01.

The blend exceeds Claude's +61.7% headline return, but fails the joint hurdle because
drawdown is much worse than −5.5%. Weight sensitivity confirmed the structural result:
25% OKX had −13.16% drawdown and 75% OKX had −13.14%. The venues express the same
market-wide cascade at almost the same times, so combining them adds exposure rather
than an independent return stream. No venue-weight optimization will follow.
